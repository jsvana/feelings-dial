Feelings Dial

Name what you feel, and notice where you feel it.

Feelings Dial is an iPhone app for putting a precise name to an emotion. Turn the dial from a core feeling, like Sad or Happy, out to more specific words, like Isolated or Thankful, and see the body sensations that often come with each one.

Support

Found a bug, or have a suggestion? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I use the dial? Drag sideways to turn it; it settles on the nearest feeling. Tap any tile to turn straight to it. The card at the top shows the feeling you've picked and where it often shows up in the body.

Does the app store anything about me? No. It collects no data and makes no network requests. See the privacy policy.

Is this medical advice? No. The body sensations are common, general descriptions, and everyone experiences emotions differently. If you're struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional, or your local emergency number in a crisis.

Where does the wheel come from? It's based on the Feelings Wheel originally created by Dr. Gloria Willcox, in its widely shared seven-core adaptation.
