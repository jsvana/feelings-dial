Privacy Policy

Feelings Dial for iPhone · Effective October 31, 2026

In short: Feelings Dial collects no data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Feelings Dial has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code. It makes no network requests. The feelings you look at are not recorded, stored, or shared.

Links

The app's About screen links to this page and to the support page. These open in your web browser and are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
